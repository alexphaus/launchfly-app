# 🚀 The $1,500/Month "AI Agency" System (Digital Nomad Edition)

**Goal:** Generate $1,500/month profit (~5 clients) by selling "Done-For-You" Lead Magnet Funnels to online coaches.
**Time Required:** 2-3 hours/day.
**Tools:** Launchfly Terminal Script (`scripts/generate-client-asset.js`), LinkedIn/Instagram, Google Docs/Canva.

---

## 🗓️ Phase 1: The Offer (The "Wedge")

You are NOT selling "software." You are selling a **result**.

**The Offer:** "The 24-Hour Lead Magnet Funnel"
1.  **High-Value PDF Guide** (Generated by your script)
2.  **High-Converting Landing Page** (Generated by Launchfly Dashboard)
3.  **Tech Setup** (Hosting & Delivery)

**Price:** **$297** (One-time)
**Target:** Online Coaches (Fitness, Business, Life, Career) who *hate* tech and writing.

---

## 🗓️ Phase 2: The Daily Process (Step-by-Step)

### Step 1: Prospecting (30 mins)
**Where:** LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook Groups.
**Search Terms:** "Fitness Coach", "Business Consultant", "Life Coach", "Career Coach".
**Criteria:**
*   Active in the last week (posted content).
*   Has a link in bio but **NO** lead magnet (or a broken/ugly one).
*   < 10k followers (Easier to reach).

### Step 2: The "Hook" Message (30 mins)
Send 10-20 DMs per day. Do NOT sell. Just offer value.

**Template 1 (The "Free Draft" Approach - Best for Beginners):**
> "Hey [Name], loved your recent post about [Topic].
>
> I'm testing a new AI workflow that builds high-end lead magnets for coaches. I’d love to generate a 20-page 'Expert Guide' on [Their Niche Topic] for you to use with your audience—completely free.
>
> No catch, just looking for a testimonial if you like it. Want me to send it over?"

**Template 2 (The "Direct Value" Approach):**
> "Hey [Name], I noticed you aren't collecting emails on your profile. You're probably leaving money on the table!
>
> I build '24-Hour Lead Magnet Funnels' for coaches. I write the guide AND build the landing page for you.
>
> I can send you a free draft of a guide I made for your niche just to show you the quality. Open to taking a look?"

### Step 3: The Fulfillment (5 mins)
When they say "Yes/Sure":

1.  **Open Terminal:**
    ```bash
    node scripts/generate-client-asset.js --type expert-guide --topic "[Their Specific Topic]" --niche "[Their Niche]" --client "[Their Name]"
    ```
2.  **Format:**
    *   Copy the text from `scripts/output/...md`.
    *   Paste into a clean Google Doc.
    *   Add a Title Page with their Name.
    *   Export as PDF.

### Step 4: The Delivery & Upsell (The "Close")
Reply with the PDF attached.

**The "Upsell" Script:**
> "Here is the guide! 🎁 I used my AI system to ensure it has 'insider' tips and actionable steps.
>
> **Quick question:** Do you have a landing page ready to capture emails for this?
>
> If not, I can build the **entire funnel** (Landing Page + Email Delivery + Hosting) for you in 24 hours.
>
> I usually charge $497, but since I already made the content, I can do the full setup for **$297**.
>
> Want me to get that live for you by tomorrow?"

### Step 5: Getting Paid
*   If they say "Yes": Send a **Stripe Payment Link** or **PayPal Link**.
*   Once paid:
    1.  Go to your Launchfly Dashboard.
    2.  "Create New Business" -> Enter their info.
    3.  Launch the site.
    4.  Send them the URL.

## 💡 "Insider" Tips for Success

1.  **Don't Act Like a Bot:** Personalize the first sentence of the DM. Mention their specific post or city.
2.  **Speed Kills:** Deliver the "Free Draft" within 1 hour if possible. The "Wow" factor of speed sells the service.
3.  **Follow Up:** If they don't reply after you send the free PDF, follow up 2 days later:
    > "Hey [Name], did you get a chance to look at that guide? I can still get that landing page up for you this week if you want to start collecting emails!"
