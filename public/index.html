FAQs

Straight Answers.

Does this replace my receptionist or admin staff?
+
It handles quote intake, supplier chasing, and follow-ups automatically — work that typically eats 2–4 hours of admin time per day. Whether you redeploy your admin or reduce hours is up to you. Most clients keep their staff focused on higher-value work like customer service and job planning.
What if my suppliers don't use WhatsApp?
+
The AI can reach out via WhatsApp, SMS, or email depending on how your suppliers prefer to communicate. During your setup phase, we map each supplier's preferred channel and configure the AI to match.
How long does setup take?
+
Most Done-For-You setups are fully live within 7–10 business days. This includes loading your supplier database, integrating your WhatsApp number, and training the AI on your typical job types and pricing margins.
What happens if the AI makes a mistake on a quote?
+
You always have the final approval before any purchase order is sent or any quote is committed. The AI drafts, you approve. You can also set hard guardrails (price thresholds, supplier whitelists) to prevent any out-of-range actions.
Is there a lock-in contract?
+
Monthly subscriptions are month-to-month. The setup fee is a one-time cost that covers our build work. If you cancel within 60 days and the guarantee conditions apply, we issue a full setup refund. After 60 days, the subscription cancels at the end of your billing period.
