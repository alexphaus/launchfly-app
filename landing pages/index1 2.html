Got Questions? We've Got Answers

Everything you need to know about launching with AI

Do I need any technical skills or experience?
Absolutely not! If you can use Facebook or send an email, you can use Launchly. Our AI handles all the technical stuff - coding, design, integrations, payment processing. You just answer a few questions about your interests and click launch. We've had successful users from ages 19 to 72, with zero tech experience.
How quickly can I really make money?
93% of our users make their first sale within 48 hours. The record is 37 minutes (a fitness coach who got a client while still customizing her site!). Our AI creates targeted marketing campaigns that start bringing traffic immediately. Some businesses see sales within hours, though 1-3 days is typical for your first customer.
What types of businesses can I create?
E-commerce stores, coaching/consulting services, online courses, digital products, service businesses, subscription boxes, affiliate sites, and more. Our AI analyzes your skills and interests to create the perfect business model for you. Popular categories include fitness coaching, business consulting, craft stores, digital marketing services, and educational platforms.
What if I don't make money? Is there a guarantee?
Yes! All plans come with a 30-day money-back guarantee. If you're not satisfied with our service within the first 30 days, we'll refund you completely. Our Growth plan includes a $1,000 revenue guarantee - if you don't hit that in 90 days, we work with you for free until you do. The Scale plan guarantees $10K/month within 6 months.
How is this different from website builders like Wix or Shopify?
Website builders give you empty tools - you still have to figure out what to sell, how to price it, write all the copy, create products, set up marketing, find customers, etc. Launchly gives you a complete, ready-to-profit business. Our AI creates your products, writes your copy, sets up your funnels, launches marketing campaigns, and brings you customers. It's like hiring a team of experts vs. getting a toolbox.
Can I customize what the AI creates?
Yes! While the AI does the heavy lifting, you have full control to customize everything - from design and branding to products and pricing. Most users find the AI's choices are spot-on (it analyzes successful businesses in your niche), but you can always tweak. Think of it as getting a 95% finished business that you can polish to perfection.
