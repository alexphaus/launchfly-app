## 🚀 How to Enable (30 Seconds)

1. Click **Settings** in your dashboard
2. Enter your phone number (e.g., +1 555-123-4567)
3. Click **Save**
4. That's it! SMS notifications are ON by default ✅

## 📲 Managing Notifications

### Turn On/Off Anytime
Just toggle the SMS Notifications switch in Settings. No commitment required!

### Update Your Number
Change your phone number anytime in Settings.

### It's Free to You!
SMS notifications are included with your Launchfly account. No extra charge!

1. **Use International Format**: Include country code (+1 for US)

2. **Enable Before Launch**: Set it up before you launch so you don't miss your first sale!
