### **Personal Touch:**
- Personalize every message
- Use their name
- Reference their business
- Build relationship, not just sell

### **Social Proof:**
- Share customer results
- Get testimonials early
- Create case studies
- Show real numbers

- Build trust first

- Don't give up after first "no"
- Follow up weekly
- Stay top of mind
